Terms, the short version

This page is the summary. The signed agreement is the document that counts.

Draft — lawyer review pending. This summary is version 0.1, dated 21 September 2026, and the agreement behind it has not been reviewed by a solicitor. Nothing on this page creates a contract. You will get the full terms to read before you pay anything, and the proposal you accept wins on scope and price.

The ten-line version

  1. Who you are contracting with. Marcus Derbali, ABN 80 873 771 166, a sole trader trading as Tradie Office. Governing law: South Australia.
  2. What the service is. Quote packs built from your job lines; quote follow-ups on a cadence across text and email; every lead answered; bookings written into your job system and calendar; a review ask on the day you finish; and one daily approval email that gates all of it. Plus a morning digest, a needs-attention sweep and a content engine.
  3. What it is not. Not a call answering service, not an employee, not a guarantee of work won. It does not give trade advice, does not put a price in a message, and does not contact your customers without your approval.
  4. Price. $3,000 setup one-off and $800 per month, both AUD inc GST. First invoice $3,800. Invoices due 7 days from issue.
  5. Term. Six-month minimum from go-live, then month to month on 30 days' written notice either way. The setup fee is refundable until install work starts, and not after. We can change the monthly fee on 60 days' notice, never during your minimum term.
  6. The 30-day guarantee. For 30 days after go-live, if it has not answered every lead that came in through a connected channel, we keep working free until it does. It is a promise of work, not a refund, and it does not cover leads that never reached a connected channel or messages you chose not to approve.
  7. What you have to give us. Access to GoHighLevel, your job system, Gmail, your review link, your rate card and hours, and a decision-maker who reads the approval email on business days. Keep the credentials current — almost every outage is a token that quietly stopped working.
  8. You are the sender. Under the Spam Act 2003 you are the sender of every commercial message the service sends, and you warrant you have consent. The service identifies your business in every message, carries a working opt-out, honours opt-outs it can see and holds messages outside your quiet hours. That is assistance, not compliance.
  9. Your data. You own it. We use it only to deliver the service, never to market to your customers and never to serve another client. Named subprocessors are Anthropic, GoHighLevel/LeadConnector, ServiceM8, Google, Telegram and Stripe. On termination we disconnect, export on request, and delete after 30 days.
  10. Liability. Nothing excludes your rights under the Australian Consumer Law. Beyond that, no indirect or consequential loss, total liability capped at the fees paid in the six months before a claim, and we are not liable for a third-party platform revoking access or for a message you approved.

What we do not claim

We do not promise a number of jobs, a revenue figure, or a percentage increase in anything. If you see a claim like that anywhere with our name on it, it did not come from us.
