The deal

  • Six-month minimum term from go-live, then month to month with 30 days' notice.
  • The install takes 14 days from the kick-off call.
  • Founding customers, being the first ten businesses, keep the founding price for as long as they stay.

The 30-day guarantee

Thirty days after go-live we check whether every lead that came in got an answer. If any did not, we keep working on it free until they do. It is a promise about answering, not a promise of more work and not a refund.

What we are responsible for

Building it, installing it, keeping it running, and fixing it when it breaks. Every message it writes is checked before it reaches your approval queue.

What you are responsible for

  • Approving what goes out. You are the sender. Read before you tap yes.
  • Your own ServiceM8, CRM, Google and messaging accounts and their fees.
  • Your obligations under Australian consumer, spam and privacy law when you message your customers.
  • Your electrical, plumbing or other trade licensing and the work itself. We do not touch that.

What we do not claim

We do not promise a number of jobs, a revenue figure, or a percentage increase in anything. If you see a claim like that anywhere with our name on it, it did not come from us.
