Questions, answered straight.

Including the ones that make us look less clever than we'd like.

How does the quote chasing actually work?

You price the job in ServiceM8. The pack goes out — the quote PDF, a spec sheet per product, photos of work like theirs — and a text says it landed.

Then it chases. Day 3 and day 8 by default, across text and email, and four touches on the bigger jobs. Before every single chase it reads both channels, so someone who already replied, already booked or already said no is never chased. Every chase waits in your approval email first.

Each chase message names your business and carries a working opt-out, because under the Spam Act a follow-up text is a commercial message and you are the sender. If someone opts out they are suppressed everywhere, straight away.

Isn't this just an AI receptionist?

No, and we'd rather say so than let you find out later.

An AI receptionist answers the phone. That is now a cheap add-on on software you may already pay for, and it is not worth three grand on its own.

What it does not do is open your job, build the quote pack, chase that quote on a cadence across two channels, write the booking into your diary, and hand you one email a day to approve the lot. That whole loop is what you are buying here.

Does it send messages without me?

No. Not on day one, and not unless you choose it.

Out of the box every message waits in an approval queue. You get one email a day listing every item with the exact words, and a Telegram nudge so you know it's there. Nothing goes out until you tap approve.

After a path has run 20 times without you changing a word, you may switch that one path to hands-off. Most people do that with review asks first. Anything to do with money can never be switched to hands-off.

Does it quote prices?

It builds your quote. It does not put a price in a message.

There is a hard rule in the system: no dollar figure appears in any SMS or email it writes. Prices live in one place only, the quote PDF you approved. That rule is a check the message has to pass before it can even reach your approval queue, not a guideline we hope it follows.

The prices in the PDF come from the lines you entered in ServiceM8. It does not invent a number and it does not discount.

What if I don't use ServiceM8?

Then two of the six features are reduced, and we'll tell you that before you pay.

Lead answering, follow-ups, review asks and approvals all work without it. Quote packs and automatic bookings are built on ServiceM8, so without it you'd be sending us your quote lines another way, and bookings would go to Google Calendar only.

We have not built adapters for simPRO, Tradify or AroFlo yet. If you're on one of those, apply anyway and say so. We'll be honest about whether it's worth your money today.

Who owns my data?

You do. All of it.

Your customer records stay in your own ServiceM8, your own CRM and your own Google account. We work inside your accounts using access you grant and can revoke. We keep a log of what was drafted and what you approved, so there's a record of what left your business.

If you leave, we hand back the log, remove our access, and delete what we hold. We do not sell your data, we do not share it with other customers, and we do not use your customers' messages to train anything we sell to someone else.

Does it sound like a robot?

It's written to sound like your business, because on day 10 of the install you read every draft and change the lines that don't sound like you.

It writes short. Plain English, no exclamation marks, no "we value your enquiry". If a customer asks something it doesn't know, it says it will find out rather than making something up.

Will it answer my phone?

No. There is no voice on the line.

If you miss a call, it sends a text straight back within about a minute, asking what the job is and where. Most people reply to that text. We'd rather do that well than have a robot voice annoy your customers.

What happens when a customer asks something it can't handle?

It stops and hands it to you.

Anything it isn't sure about goes to you as a flagged item rather than a drafted reply. It will not guess at stock, at a delivery date, at whether you can be there Thursday, or at what something costs.

Can I cancel?

There's a six-month minimum term, then it's month to month with 30 days' notice.

The minimum is there because the setup is done by hand and it takes a couple of months before you can fairly judge it. We're not going to pretend otherwise. If we've failed the 30-day guarantee, that's on us and we keep working free until it's fixed.

What is the 30-day guarantee, exactly?

Thirty days after you go live, we check whether every lead that came in got an answer. If any didn't, we keep working on it free until they do.

Read what it is not: it is not a promise of more jobs, a revenue figure, or a refund. It's a promise about answering, which is the thing we actually control.

How much of my time does the install take?

About two hours spread over the fortnight.

A 45-minute kick-off call on day 1, half an hour reading the drafts on day 10, a 30-minute walkthrough on day 13, and a few minutes here and there clicking approve on account connections. We do the rest.

Is my customers' information safe with an AI?

Here's the honest version. The assistant uses a commercial AI model to write drafts, so the text of an enquiry is processed by that model to produce a reply. We do not send it your whole customer database, and the drafts come back to your approval queue rather than going out.

You'll get this in writing in the terms before you sign, including which parts of a message are processed and which never leave your systems. If that's a dealbreaker for you, tell us at the call, not after.

Why should I trust you?

Because I'm a licensed electrician who built this for my own business first. I run Amped Up Electrical Adelaide, which has more than 80 five-star Google reviews, and I used this system to get a fair few of them.

I'm not going to show you a wall of testimonials from customers I don't have yet. We're taking ten founding customers. You'd be one of the first, and the price reflects that.
