What happens next

  1. We read it, usually the same day.
  2. If it looks like a fit, we text you to book a 30-minute call.
  3. On the call we go through your enquiries, honestly, and tell you whether this is worth your money.
  4. If yes, the 14-day install starts the following Monday.

We'll say no if

  • You're not getting enquiries yet. This works the leads you have. It doesn't create them.
  • You want it to quote prices by text. It won't, and we won't change that.
  • You want it fully hands-off from day one. That's not how we start.
