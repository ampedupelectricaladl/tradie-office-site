Privacy, the short version

This page is the summary. The full policy is the document that counts.

Draft — lawyer review pending. This summary is version 0.1, dated 21 September 2026, and it has not yet been reviewed by a solicitor. The full policy is the document that counts and you will get it in writing before you pay anything. If a point here matters to your decision, ask us in writing and we will answer in writing.

The ten-line version

  1. Who we are. Marcus Derbali, ABN 80 873 771 166, a sole trader in Adelaide, South Australia, trading as Tradie Office.
  2. The small business exemption. We are under the $3 million Privacy Act threshold, so parts of the Act may not bind us yet. We are written to the Australian Privacy Principles anyway, and nothing changes if we cross the threshold.
  3. Two kinds of people. You, the trade business owner, whose information we hold ourselves; and your customers, whose information we handle only on your behalf and on your instructions.
  4. What we collect from you. Name, business name, email, mobile, trade, suburb, team size, job volume, which systems you use, your public business details, billing details through Stripe, access tokens you grant, and our support history with you. No sensitive information — please don't send any.
  5. What we handle about your customers. The records already in your systems: name, phone, email, address or suburb, the enquiry and photos, message history, quote, booking and job status, review requests.
  6. What we never do with it. We do not sell it, market our own things to your customers, use it to train a general model, profile them for anyone else, or use one client's data to serve another.
  7. AI. Drafts are generated by a large language model (Anthropic). The relevant parts of an enquiry go to that model's API. Content sent there is not used to train it. Drafts wait for your approval.
  8. Who else touches it. Anthropic, GoHighLevel/LeadConnector, ServiceM8, Google (Gmail and Calendar), Telegram and Stripe. Several hold data outside Australia, mainly the United States. We tell you before adding another that handles your customers' information.
  9. How long. Applications that go nowhere: 12 months. Your account: while you are a customer. Your customers' data in our systems: deleted 30 days after the agreement ends. Tokens: revoked at termination. Billing and tax records: 7 years. AI usage logs, which hold no message content: 12 months.
  10. Your rights. Ask us to show, correct, export or delete what we hold, and we respond within 30 days. If you are a homeowner whose enquiry was handled by this service, contact the trade business you dealt with — they hold the relationship and instruct us. Complaints can go to the OAIC, oaic.gov.au, 1300 363 992.
