Privacy, the short version

This page is the summary. The full policy is the document that counts.

Placeholder notice. The full privacy policy is being finalised and will be published at docs/PRIVACY.md in this project and linked from this page. Until that link is live, this summary is a description of how we intend to operate and is not a substitute for the full policy. If anything here matters to your decision, ask us in writing before you sign and we will answer in writing.

What we collect from this website

Only what you type into the application form: business name, trade, your name, mobile, email, city, team size, and what you wrote about your biggest headache.

We do not run advertising trackers or analytics scripts on this site. There are no third-party fonts or scripts loaded from anywhere else.

What we do with it

We use it to reply to you about Tradie Office and to work out whether it's a fit. That's all. We do not sell it, rent it, or hand it to a list broker.

Your customers' information

If you become a customer, Tradie Office works inside your own accounts — your ServiceM8, your CRM, your Google account. Those records stay yours. We access them with permission you grant and can revoke at any time.

We keep a log of what was drafted, what you approved and what was sent, so there is a record of what left your business. You get a copy of that log if you leave.

AI processing

The assistant uses a commercial AI model to draft replies, so the text of an enquiry is processed by that model. We do not send your customer database to it. Drafts come back to your approval queue, not to a customer.

Ask us anything

Email hello@tradieoffice.com.au and ask for a copy of what we hold about you, or ask us to delete it. We'll do it.
