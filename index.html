Why not just an AI receptionist?

An AI receptionist answers the phone. That's a real job and it's worth something — it is also a $29-a-month add-on on software you may already pay for. We are not going to charge you three grand for it.

Here's the difference, in plain words. A receptionist picks up. It does not open your job in ServiceM8 and build the quote. It does not send that quote as a pack with a spec sheet. It does not come back on day 3 and day 8 across two channels to get you an answer. And it does not ask you before it sends.

Tradie Office is the loop after the phone call: quote it, pack it, chase it, book it, ask for the review — and hand you one email a day to say yes or no to the lot.

And every chase is a legal message

Under the Spam Act, a follow-up text is a commercial message. So every one we send names your business and carries a working opt-out, and an opt-out is honoured and suppressed everywhere.

You are the sender. We build it so that's a safe thing to be.

What it does not do

Read this bit twice. It is the part most people skip.

  • It does not quote a price in a message. No dollar figure goes in a text or an email. Ever. Prices live in the PDF quote you approved.
  • It does not send without you. Out of the box, every single message waits for your tick.
  • It does not answer the phone in a voice. If you miss a call, it sends a text straight back. It does not pretend to be a person on the line.
  • It does not find you new leads. It works the leads you already get. If nobody is calling you, this is the wrong tool.
  • It does not do your books. No BAS, no payroll, no bank reconciliation.
  • It does not promise a callback on your behalf. It will not say "Marcus will call you at 4" unless that is a booking that actually exists.
  • It is not instant. The install takes 14 days because we set it up around your business, not a template.
