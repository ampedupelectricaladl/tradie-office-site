What it does not do

Read this bit twice. It is the part most people skip.

  • It does not quote a price in a message. No dollar figure goes in a text or an email. Ever. Prices live in the PDF quote you approved.
  • It does not send without you. Out of the box, every single message waits for your tick.
  • It does not answer the phone in a voice. If you miss a call, it sends a text straight back. It does not pretend to be a person on the line.
  • It does not find you new leads. It works the leads you already get. If nobody is calling you, this is the wrong tool.
  • It does not do your books. No BAS, no payroll, no bank reconciliation.
  • It does not promise a callback on your behalf. It will not say "Marcus will call you at 4" unless that is a booking that actually exists.
  • It is not instant. The install takes 14 days because we set it up around your business, not a template.
